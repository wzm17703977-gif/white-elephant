Secret Santa Generator — No Email, No Sign-Up

Type the names, add any “can’t draw each other” pairs, and get one private link per person. Send the links by text or WhatsApp. Nobody draws themselves, and you don’t see who got whom.

Send each link only to the person named next to it. Don’t open the links yourself if you’re playing — they reveal the match. Your draw is saved in this browser, so you can come back and copy links again.

    How it works

    1. Add names. Duplicates are removed automatically.
    2. Add exclusions for people who shouldn’t draw each other.
    3. Tap Draw names. The draw happens on your device using a fair random shuffle. Nobody draws themselves and every exclusion is respected.
    4. Send the links. Tap Text, Share or Copy next to each name. When someone opens their link, they see only who they’re buying for, plus the budget, date and note.

    FAQ

    Do participants need an email or an account?

    No. Nobody signs up — not you, not them. Each person just opens a link.

    Can the organizer see who got whom?

    The pairs are never shown on screen; you only see each giver’s name and their link. The links are scrambled but not encrypted, so a determined, tech-savvy organizer could decode them. If that matters for your group, ask someone who isn’t playing to run the draw.

    Is anything stored on a server?

    No. The names and the draw stay in your browser, and the match travels inside each person’s link. There’s no database to leak.

    What if the draw is impossible?

    With too many exclusions some groups can’t be matched (for example, three people who all exclude each other). The generator tells you, and you can remove an exclusion and try again.

    How does my Secret Santa know what to buy?

    Send everyone a Secret Santa questionnaire first — favorite colors, snacks, sizes, and what to avoid. It prints, copies into a group chat, or works as a fill-in link.

    Playing white elephant instead?

    Use the white elephant number generator for turn order, or the dice game for a faster, sillier version.
