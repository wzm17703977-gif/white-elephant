How it works

  1. Pick a question set — Coworkers keeps it work-friendly, Family adds clothing and shoe sizes, Kids asks about characters and toys. Tick or untick any question and add your own.
  2. Choose how to share it. Print blank sheets to hand out at the office, copy the plain-text list into a chat or Google Form, or create one fill-in link for everyone.
  3. Collect the answers. People who used the link send you back an answer link. Paste them all into step 4 and print one page with everyone’s answers — handy when you hand out the Secret Santa assignments.

Good Secret Santa questions to ask

The best questionnaires are short — ten to twelve questions is plenty. Ask about favorite colors, hobbies, snacks and drinks, where they like to shop, something small they’d love, and always include allergies and a “please don’t get me…” line. For family exchanges, clothing and shoe sizes save a lot of returns. For coworkers, skip sizes and add “something that would make your desk better.”

FAQ

Is it really free? Do people need an account?

Yes, it’s free, and nobody signs up — not you, not your group. Everything runs in your browser.

Where are the answers stored?

Nowhere on a server. The questions travel inside the fill-in link, and each person’s answers travel inside their answer link. The links are encoded, not encrypted, so anyone who has a link can read it — only share answer links with your organizer.

Can I use it with Google Forms?

Yes. Tap Copy question list and paste it into a new Google Form, one question per line. Or skip the form entirely and use the fill-in link.

Can I print a PDF?

Tap Print blank questionnaire and choose “Save as PDF” in the print dialog. Use “2 per page” to save paper.
