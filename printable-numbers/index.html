Printable White Elephant Numbers

Choose how many players you have and a card style, then print. Cut along the dashed lines, fold, and draw from a hat.

Tip: in the print dialog choose “Save as PDF” to keep a copy.

How to use the number cards

  1. Print one card per player. Bold green looks great but uses more ink — pick Plain for a home printer.
  2. Cut along the dashed lines and fold each card in half so the number is hidden.
  3. Drop them in a hat or bowl. Everyone draws one; number 1 goes first.

No printer? Use the white elephant number generator and pass a phone around instead. New to the game? Read the white elephant rules.
