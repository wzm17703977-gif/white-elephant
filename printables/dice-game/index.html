White Elephant Dice Game Printable

Two free rules sheets: the classic one-die game and the two-dice doubles game. Print one, tape it to the wall, and grab a die.

In the print dialog you can also choose “Save as PDF”.

White Elephant Dice Game

Everyone holds a wrapped gift. On your turn, roll one die and do what it says.

One die

    Timer: play for 10 minutes. When time is up, unwrap the gift you are holding. It’s yours.

    Free printable from Gift Swap Kitwzm17703977-gif.github.io/white-elephant

    White Elephant Doubles Game

    Gifts go in the middle. Sit in a circle and pass two dice around.

    Two dice

    Round 1: fill your hands

    Roll doubles: take a wrapped gift from the pile.

    No doubles: pass the dice to the next person.

    Keep going until every gift has been taken.

    Round 2: steal (10-minute timer)

    Roll doubles: steal any gift, or swap any two gifts.

    No doubles: pass the dice.

    When the timer ends, unwrap what you are holding.

    Tip: use two pairs of dice for groups over 15 so the game keeps moving.

    Free printable from Gift Swap Kitwzm17703977-gif.github.io/white-elephant

    How to use these sheets

    1. Pick the one-die game for a short, lively round, or the doubles game when you want everyone waiting on luck.
    2. Print on letter paper. On A4, choose “Fit to page” in the print dialog.
    3. No dice at home? Roll on your phone with the online white elephant dice game. It has a timer and both modes.
