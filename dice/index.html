How the doubles game works

Round 1: gifts sit in the middle. Roll doubles and take a gift from the pile; otherwise pass the dice. Keep going until every gift is taken.

Round 2: start the timer. Doubles now mean steal any gift, or swap any two. When time is up, unwrap what you hold.

How to play the white elephant dice game

  1. Everyone brings one wrapped gift and holds it. Sit in a circle so “left” and “right” mean something.
  2. Pass this phone around (or put it on a TV). On your turn, tap the die and do what the number says.
  3. Start the timer. Ten minutes works well for 10–15 people; add a few minutes for bigger groups.
  4. When the timer ends, unwrap whatever you’re holding. That gift is yours.

FAQ

Is there an official set of dice rules?

No. Families and offices all play slightly different versions. The defaults here are a common, easy set; tap Edit moves to change any number.

Can we play with two dice?

Yes. Switch to Two dice (doubles) above. You only act when you roll doubles, which happens about once every six rolls, so the game builds suspense. Both dice are fair and rolled separately.

Does it work offline?

Once the page has loaded, rolling and the timer keep working without a connection. Your edited moves are saved in this browser.
