How to use it at your party

  1. Count the players (or paste everyone’s names) and tap Generate order.
  2. Reveal the order: tap Reveal on big screen and show one number at a time on a TV or laptop — it builds suspense.
  3. Play: number 1 opens a gift. Each next player either opens a new gift or steals one that is already open. Need a refresher? Read the white elephant rules.
  4. Want more chaos? Switch to the white elephant dice game, where a roll decides whether you open, steal or swap.

FAQ

How do you pick numbers for white elephant?

Every player draws a number at random. Number 1 goes first, number 2 second, and so on. This generator does the draw for you, so nobody has to cut up paper — though you can still print number cards if you like the hat tradition.

Is it an advantage to be number 1?

Usually not: number 1 opens a gift that everyone else can steal. A popular fix is to give number 1 a final turn at the very end to swap with anyone.

What is the difference between white elephant and Yankee swap?

They are the same game with different names. “Yankee swap” is common in New England, “Dirty Santa” in the South. The number draw works the same way for all of them.

Can I use real names instead of numbers?

Yes. Switch to By names, paste one name per line, and the order will show names next to their numbers. Duplicate names are removed automatically.

Is my list saved or uploaded?

Your last list is saved only in this browser so a refresh doesn’t lose it. Nothing is sent to a server, and there is no sign-up.
